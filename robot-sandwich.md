How to make a peanut butter sandwich.

You are standing in a kitchen facing a large clean counter.
The items on it are listed below in order from left to right on the counter.
A metal butter knife with the blade facing away from you.
A half empty jar of peanut butter with a twist off lid. The lid is on and must turn counter clockwise to come off.
A tall resealable plastic box containing 10 slices of bread. The lid just pops off, it doesn't twist. The bread slices are laying in the container horizontally so the crust does not face up or down.
An empty plate.

1. Grab the base of the bread box with your left hand to prevent it from moving.
2. Grab the lid of the bread box with your right hand and lift it straight up off of the box.
3. Move your right hand to the right of the plate.
4. Release your grip on the lid.
5. Move your right hand into the top of the bread box slowly until you feel bread.
6 Once you feel bread, place your fingers on the outer side of the bread slice, make sure your finger nails are facing away from the center of the bread slice.
7 Begin to slightly wiggle your fingers.
8 Gently begin to push your fingers into where the top 2 bread slices meet.
9. Once your right hand in fully between the two bread slices, stop wiggling your fingers.
10. Adjust the angle of your right hand to be parallel to the counter with your palm facing up.
11. Lift your right hand out of the box.
12. Move your right hand over the right side of the plate.
13. Slowly rotate your hand so your palm faces down.
14. Repeat steps 5 - 11
15. Move your right hand over the left side of the plate.
16. Slowly rotate your hand so your palm faces down.
17. Grab the side of the lid of the bread box with your right hand.
18. Move the lid over the bread box.
19. Rotate the lid until it settles onto the bread box.
20. Release your grip on the bread box lid.
21. Push down on each corner of the bread box lid until you've head a quiet snap.
22. Release your grip on the bread box.

23. Grab the base of the peanut butter jar with your left hand.
24. Grab the lid of the peanut butter with your right hand.
25. Turn the lid in a way that it would be rotating counter clockwise if you were looking at it from the ceiling.
26. Lift the lid off of the peanut butter.
27. Move your right hand to the left of the knife.
28. Release your grip on the peanut butter lid.
29. Grab the handle of the butter knife.
30. Insert the blade of the knife into the peanut butter, make sure the blade is facing you and is closer to you than the other side of the peanut butter jar.
31. Twist the peanut butter jar 180 degrees counter clockwise.
32. Lift the knife out of the peanut butter.
33. Rotate the knife 90 degrees clockwise so that the knife is between the counter and the peanut butter.
34. Release your grip on the peanut butter jar.
35. Move your hand under the knife in case the peanut butter falls.
36. Slowly move right until the knife is hovering over the left piece of bread on the plate.
37. Grab the side of the plate with your left hand.
38. Lower the knife until it is almost touching the bread slice.
39. Rotate the knife 180 degrees.
40. Lightly shake the knife left and right until the peanut butter falls off.
41. Once the peanut butter falls, press the knife against the left side of the peanut butter glob.
42. Slowly move the knife forward and back.
43. Slowly move the knife to the right.
44. Stop when the peanut butter gets close to the end of the left bread slice.
45. Move the knife left and up to escape the peanut butter.
46. Place the knife on the counter.
47. Pick up the bread slice that doesn't have peanut butter on it.
48. Move it over the bread slice that does have peanut butter on it.
49. Let go of the bread slice.
50. The sandwich is complete, I'll handle the mess you've created.
